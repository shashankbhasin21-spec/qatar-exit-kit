For expats leaving Qatar permanently (especially Indians returning home). The order matters: settle bank debts early, transfer your car before your residence permit (RP) is cancelled, cancel dependents’ RPs before the sponsor’s, keep one bank account open for refunds, and leave within the grace period. This kit is a plain checklist and date helper — not a filing service and not legal or immigration advice.

Privacy: nothing you type leaves this device. No accounts, analytics or cookies.

Not legal, immigration, labour or tax advice. Qatar rules change. Always confirm on your RP cancellation document and on official channels (MOI portal / Metrash, Kahramaa, your bank, your employer’s PRO). Exit-permit rules depend on your category — check yours.

Why this exists

  • Independent guides report a grace period of 30 days after RP cancellation for residents (7 days for visit visas) before fines apply — your cancellation paper is the final word.
  • Since Qatar’s 2019–2020 reforms, most expatriate employees no longer need an exit permit, but some categories still do — check MOI / Metrash for yours.
  • Common traps: selling the car after the RP is cancelled, closing the last bank account before the gratuity or Kahramaa refund arrives, leaving postpaid telecom open, and unpaid loans or cards that can block travel.
  • Indian returnees also need India-side paperwork. For passport / OCI / PCC deadlines, see our sister kit Mohlat.
